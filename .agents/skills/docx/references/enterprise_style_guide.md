# Enterprise Document Design Guide (World-Class Standard)

This guide documents the design standards used by top-tier consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG, Bain), investment banks, and technology corporations for high-impact reports, proposals, and deliverables.

---

## 🎨 1. Corporate Color Palettes

### Palette A: Classic Corporate (Big 4 / Strategy Consulting)
- **Primary Header / Brand**: `#0F2C59` (Deep Oxford Navy)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#005B94` (Executive Blue)
- **Highlight / Callout Accent**: `#D97706` (Amber Gold) or `#2563EB` (Royal Blue)
- **Body Text**: `#1F2937` (Slate Charcoal, never pure black `#000000`)
- **Muted / Captions**: `#6B7280` (Cool Gray)
- **Background Fills (Tables/Boxes)**: `#F8FAFC` (Ice White) & `#F1F5F9` (Soft Slate)

### Palette B: Modern Technology & FinTech
- **Primary**: `#0F172A` (Obsidian Navy)
- **Accent**: `#2563EB` (Electric Cobalt)
- **Success / Positive KPI**: `#059669` (Emerald)
- **Warning / Alert**: `#D97706` (Amber)
- **Subtle Surface**: `#F8FAFC`

### Palette C: Academic & Government Formal (Vietnam & Global)
- **Primary**: `#1A365D` (Midnight Navy) or `#7F1D1D` (Crimson Burgundy)
- **Accent**: `#2B6CB0` (Steel Blue)
- **Body Text**: `#111827` (Deep Ink)
- **Table Header**: `#1A365D` with White text (`#FFFFFF`)

## 📐 2. Typography & Hierarchy Standard

| Level | Size | Weight | Line Spacing | Space Before | Space After | Color | Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Document Title** | 22–24 pt | Bold | 1.15 | 18 pt | 6 pt | Primary | Center / Left |
| **Subtitle / Slogan** | 12–13 pt | Regular/Italic | 1.15 | 0 pt | 18 pt | Muted Gray | Center / Left |
| **Heading 1 (H1)** | 15–16 pt | Bold | 1.15 | 16 pt | 6 pt | Primary | Left (Keep with next) |
| **Heading 2 (H2)** | 13–14 pt | Semi-Bold | 1.15 | 12 pt | 4 pt | Secondary | Left (Keep with next) |
| **Heading 3 (H3)** | 11.5–12 pt | Semi-Bold | 1.15 | 8 pt | 2 pt | Body Text | Left (Keep with next) |
| **Body Paragraph** | 10.5–11.5 pt | Regular | 1.15–1.25 | 0 pt | 5–6 pt | Slate Text | Justified or Left |
| **Callout Content** | 10 pt | Regular | 1.15 | 0 pt | 2 pt | Primary/Dark | Left |
| **Table Cells** | 9–10 pt | Regular | 1.05 | 0 pt | 0 pt | Body Text | Left (Numbers Right) |
| **Footer / Page Num** | 9 pt | Regular | 1.0 | 0 pt | 0 pt | Muted Gray | Right / Center |

**Recommended Fonts**:
- Modern Business: `Segoe UI`, `Aptos`, `Arial`, `Inter`
- Traditional / Academic: `Times New Roman`, `Georgia`, `Calibri`

## 📊 3. Table Formatting Principles

1. **Header Row**:
   - Dark background fill (`#0F2C59` or `#1E3A8A`).
   - Bold white text (`#FFFFFF`).
   - Centered vertically, padded with 120–160 twips (6–8pt) top/bottom margin.
2. **Alternating Rows (Zebra Striping)**:
   - Odd rows: White (`#FFFFFF`).
   - Even rows: Soft light gray (`#F8FAFC` or `#F1F5F9`).
3. **Data Alignment**:
   - Text & Descriptions: Left-aligned.
   - IDs, Status, Codes, Dates: Centered.
   - Financial Numbers & Quantities: Right-aligned.
4. **Borders**:
   - Thin subtle horizontal dividers (`#E2E8F0`), omit vertical gridlines for modern editorial clarity.

---

## 📌 4. Executive Callouts & KPI Stat Cards

- **Callout Box**: 1x1 table with thick left border (36 sz, 4.5pt) colored in Accent Blue/Amber, filled with `#F8FAFC`.
- **KPI Highlight Box**: A 2x2 or 3x1 mini table showing major metrics (e.g. `99.9% Uptime`, `+45% Efficiency`, `0.15s Latency`) with giant bold numbers (18–24pt).

---

## 📄 5. Cover Page & Structure Hierarchy

Enterprise Deliverable Flow:
1. **Executive Cover Page** (Title, Document Code, Client/University, Authors, Version, Date)
2. **Executive Summary / Abstract** (In a stylized callout frame)
3. **Table of Contents (Mục Lục)**
4. **Main Body Sections (H1 - H3)**
5. **Tables, Architecture Diagrams & Process Flows**
6. **Risk & Mitigation Matrix**
7. **Appendices & References**
